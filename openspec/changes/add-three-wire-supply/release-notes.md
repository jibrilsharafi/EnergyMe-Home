## Three-phase supply without neutral

- **3×230 V without neutral:** buildings with 230 V between the lines and no neutral are now measured correctly. Turn on "No neutral (3×230 V)" in the three-phase step of the setup page, or next to the supply phase on the channel page. Before, the supply lines read about twice the real power.
- **Downgrade:** an older firmware ignores the setting and goes back to the old readings.
