Single-phase
One live wire and neutral, 230 V. Most homes. Main breaker with 2 poles.
Three-phase
Three live wires L1, L2, L3 and neutral, 400 V between them. Main breaker with 4 poles.
Split-phase
North America. Two 120 V legs, 240 V across both.

The meter's N always goes to neutral, never to another phase. Split-phase: the centre neutral.
