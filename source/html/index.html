🏠 Self-Consumption
Self-Consumption = share of solar production used directly on-site.

Formula: (Production − Grid Export) ÷ Production

100 % means all generated energy was consumed locally; 0 % means everything was exported to the grid.

🌱 Autosufficiency
Autosufficiency = share of home consumption covered by own production (solar / battery).

Formula: (Home Consumption − Grid Import) ÷ Home Consumption

100 % means the home was fully self-powered; 0 % means all energy came from the grid.
